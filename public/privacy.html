Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the CGU Automated Academic Work Tracker ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

Our application requests access to the following Google Workspace data via OAuth scopes:

  • Gmail (Read-only): To parse incoming academic announcements and extract deadlines.
  • Google Classroom (Read-only): To sync your active courses, assignments, and coursework deadlines.
  • Google Calendar (Read-only): To view your current schedule and detect conflicts with new academic tasks.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for providing the core functionality of the application:

  • To automatically create, update, and manage your academic task list.
  • To notify you of conflicting deadlines or schedule changes.
  • To provide a centralized dashboard of your academic responsibilities.

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or any other unrelated purposes. The processing (e.g., text parsing) is done securely to extract relevant academic metadata.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your extracted tasks and metadata are stored securely in our cloud database (Firebase Firestore) to provide cross-session persistence. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information or Google Workspace data to outside parties. Your data is strictly used within the application to provide you with the tracking service.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can revoke our application's access to your Google Workspace data at any time by visiting your Google Account's Security settings and removing access for this application.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the platform support channels.
